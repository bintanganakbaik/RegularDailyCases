THE STOLEN NECKLACE

Active Investigation Briefing

CASE TYPE Robbery
DIFFICULTY Easy
ESTIMATED TIME 15–20 minutes

CASE BRIEFING

"Ms. Emma's cherished necklace, valued at $9,000, has gone missing from her office desk. She only removed the necklace when necessary, and this morning she left it on her desk while using the restroom. When she returned, the necklace was gone."

INVESTIGATION OBJECTIVES

  • 01 Investigate the crime scene.
  • 02 Collect and analyze evidence.
  • 03 Identify potential suspects.
  • 04 Determine who stole the necklace.

INITIAL SUSPECTS

Ms. Emma

The victim and owner of the stolen necklace. She is meticulous and organized and takes great care of her belongings.

Mr. John

A colleague of Ms. Emma who has praised the necklace on several occasions. He has a history of financial troubles.

Ms. Sarah

Another colleague who was present in the office during the time of the theft. She is known to envy other people's possessions and once mocked Ms. Emma's necklace.
